# AI Explorers

AI Explorers is a learning platform that helps young people understand artificial intelligence through short lessons, practical examples and interactive challenges.

## Who it is for

The platform has two learning paths, developed in parallel:

- **Ages 5–9:** simple explanations, familiar examples and guided discovery.
- **Ages 10–15:** deeper explanations, technical concepts, practical applications and critical thinking.

Both paths cover the same core AI topics. The content, examples and level of detail change to suit the learner.

## Learning approach

The goal is not only to explain AI, but to help learners recognize it in everyday life. Lessons encourage four questions:

- Is AI involved here?
- What is the AI doing?
- How does it know what to do?
- Can it make mistakes?

## Topics

- AI in everyday life
- Machine learning and training data
- Finding patterns
- Neural networks
- Large language models
- Prompts and context
- Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)
- AI assistants and agents
- Reasoning, limitations and safety

Each lesson includes an explanation, an example, a reflection question and a short multiple-choice challenge.

Learners can also try five hands-on activities:

- **Pattern Finder:** see how limited examples can lead to a wrong prediction, then improve the examples.
- **Prompt Builder:** change the topic, audience and answer style to see how instructions shape a request.
- **RAG Notes Explorer:** select useful notes and see how missing information can lead to an incomplete answer.
- **Neural Network Playground:** adjust input signals and watch how a small network combines clues to produce an output.
- **Training and Testing:** explore why models need separate training and test examples, and why test data must stay unseen.
- **Can You Trust This Answer?:** compare confident AI claims with trusted facts and practise spotting statements that are unsupported or incorrect.

These activities are simplified learning demonstrations. They do not send learner information to an AI service or use a live AI model. The neural network and training/testing activities use fixed example calculations and predictions; they do not train a model.

## Progress

Lesson completion is saved locally in the learner's browser. The app does not ask learners to create an account or provide personal details.
